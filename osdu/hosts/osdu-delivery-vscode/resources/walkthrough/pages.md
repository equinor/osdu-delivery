# The estate beside your files

The side bar is the workbench's own navigation: every page of its menu opens in a tab of the editor and reads the control plane with your sign-in, so it shows what the GUI shows. Its sync button has the control plane read the latest commit of your repository, after you confirm.

A mapping has a preview of its own. **Preview Mapping** opens the mapping's page beside its file and reads the file: what it makes of its tables, the record it renders, and every property of its template. It follows the file as you save it.
