# The estate beside your files

The side bar's Estate menu is the workbench's own: every page opens in a tab of the editor and reads the control plane with your sign-in, so it shows what the GUI shows.

A mapping has a preview of its own. **Preview Mapping** opens the mapping's page beside its file and reads the file: what it makes of its tables, the record it renders, and every property of its template. It follows the file as you save it.
