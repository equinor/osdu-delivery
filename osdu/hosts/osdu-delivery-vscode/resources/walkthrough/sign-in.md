# Your environments, and signing in

Save each control plane you work against as an environment, with its name and its stage: development in green, test in orange, production in red. The side bar, every page and the status bar show the one in use in its colour, and you switch between them from the top of the side bar.

The browser opens on the environment's control plane. Sign in as you do in the GUI, Microsoft single sign-on included, and approve the code the editor shows. The sign-in belongs to the MCP server, so this editor, its chat and any assistant you connect share it, and it renews on its own. Each environment keeps its own sign-in, so switching back to one you signed in to needs no browser.
